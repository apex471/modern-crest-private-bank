Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 · Version 2.4

1. Overview & Commitment to Confidentiality

Modern Crest Private Wealth & Treasury Platform ("Modern Crest", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains our data collection, verification protocols, encryption methodologies, and disclosure practices in accordance with international data protection standards.

2. Information We Collect

In order to provide institutional treasury services, account tier verification, and secure liquidity access, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Identity Information: Full legal name, date of birth, citizenship, and administrative contact information.
  • Entity & Identification Numbers: Tax Identification Number (TIN/EIN), registration identifiers, or entity numbers strictly required for compliance.
  • Verification Documentation: Sample government-issued credentials and proof of address submitted for compliance evaluation and tier eligibility.
  • Technical Information: IP address, device telemetry, browser type, and cryptographic session tokens used strictly to safeguard against unauthorized access.

3. Purpose of Processing

We process collected information solely for authorized purposes, including:

  • Verifying client eligibility for higher liquidity tiers and bespoke sovereign limits.
  • Complying with regulatory obligations, anti-money laundering (AML) protocols, and Know-Your-Customer (KYC) standards.
  • Detecting and mitigating financial crime, fraudulent transactions, and unauthorized session hijacking.
  • Facilitating secure customer support and communication with our administrative directorate.

4. Data Security & Encryption

All sensitive information transmitted to Modern Crest is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) with SHA-256 cipher suites. At rest, data is protected using AES-256 encryption within isolated infrastructure environments.

5. Your Rights & Data Retention

You have the right to request access to the data we hold concerning your account, request corrections to inaccurate records, or request permanent deletion of your profile where retention is not otherwise mandated by applicable regulatory requirements. For inquiries regarding your privacy rights, contact our compliance office at compliance@moderncrestprivatebank.group.
